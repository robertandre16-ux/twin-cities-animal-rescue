Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue started because people in the community wanted to help animals that did not have homes.

Our organization helps animals find new families and gives people different ways to help, such as fostering and volunteering.

We believe that animals should be treated with kindness and given a chance to have a good life.

Our Core Values

  • We treat animals with kindness.
  • We try to be honest and trustworthy.
  • We want to help animals stay safe.
  • We believe the community can make a difference.
